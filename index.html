About

I'm an actuarial analyst at HNB Life in Colombo, working on the IFRS 17 implementation project, with a BSc (Hons) in Statistics from the University of Kelaniya.

An IFRS 17 valuation cycle is long, repetitive and unforgiving — the same files move between the same systems every month, and one mismatched column surfaces four steps later as a number nobody can explain. I execute that cycle end to end, from source data through to reported P&L, and I remove the manual steps from it one at a time. I have built and deployed more than fifteen production tools in Python and C#, extended data validation from sample checks to full coverage, and designed a workflow control system that governs the entire monthly close.

Every tool is reconciled against the process it replaces before anyone else uses it. Matching output is the only acceptable result — and that discipline, more than any single technology, is what I bring to data and automation engineering work.

Selected Work

Featured
IFRS 17 Valuation Workflow Control System

A control layer for the monthly valuation close. Structures the cycle into 7 sequenced steps over 113 checklist tasks, enforces doer, reviewer and approver sign-off at each one, captures supporting evidence, and blocks a step from starting until the previous one is approved. A management dashboard reports progress, working days per step against target, and the full audit history. Built with no premium connectors; handed over with a management deck and a 12-page technical specification.

Mortality Experience Study Engine

Replaced an Excel workbook so large it took minutes simply to open. A full study now runs in minutes once data is ready and outputs every breakdown summary rather than the handful anyone had time to produce by hand. Covers individual business, MRP/Micro, MRP Takaful, count basis and pricing basis, with breakdowns by channel, product, age band and sum assured band.

Prophet Table Comparator

Assumptions change every cycle, and the honest answer to "what changed?" used to be "we checked a few tables." This compares two sets of assumption tables and reports every differing value down to the table, column and row — turning assumption review from spot-checking into a complete audit trail.

RPT Combiner

A desktop application that groups and merges any set of identically formatted RPT files — model inputs or outputs — and exports straight to CSV or Excel. What took 20+ minutes of manual copy-paste now takes under 5 seconds. In daily use across the actuarial team, and makes no assumption about product structure.

Reconciliation & Data Utilities

Small, sharp tools for the work that fills a valuation cycle: a comparator reconciling two extracts on a key column and reporting every field-level difference, a combiner merging any number of identically structured extracts in milliseconds, column aligners and reserve extractors.
